# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-04**

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how GuestMe ("**we**," "**Guestme**," "**guestme**," "**us**," "**our**"), operated by an individual based in Lithuania, collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use guestme.eu (the "**Platform**"). GuestMe is a free platform, not operated through a registered company.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your rights, contact us at info@guestme.eu.

## 2. What Data We Collect

**Account credentials.** Your email address and password. Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password. _Required._

**Profile information.** Your display name or pseudonym, location (country and city), and Instagram username. _Required._

**Optional profile information.** Real name, bio, profile picture, and portfolio images. _Optional._

**Listing data.** Travel or guest-spot dates and locations you choose to publish. _Provided when you create a Listing._

**Technical data.** IP address, device/browser type, log data, and cookies. _Collected automatically._

We do not collect payment information — GuestMe does not process payments, as all bookings happen off-platform.

## 3. Why We Collect It (Legal Basis)

We process your data under the following GDPR legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to create your account, operate your profile, and let you publish and browse Listings.
- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for optional information you choose to provide (real name, bio, photos) and for certain cookies.
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the Platform secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with the law.

We do not use your data for marketing or advertising — GuestMe doesn't run any marketing, and there are no ads on the Platform. We also do not currently use analytics tools (e.g., no Google Analytics).

## 4. How We Use Your Data

- To create and manage your account, including email verification;
- To display your public profile and Listings to other users;
- To let other users find and contact you via your published Instagram username;
- To operate search and browsing of Listings;
- To communicate with you about your account or the Platform;
- To maintain security and prevent misuse of the Platform;
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 5. What's Public vs. Private

Your **display name, location (country/city), Instagram username, bio, profile picture, portfolio images, and Listings** are visible to other registered users of the Platform — this visibility is the core purpose of GuestMe. Your **email address and password are never shown to other users.**

If you choose to enter your real name, it will be displayed instead of a pseudonym; this is entirely your choice.

## 6. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

- **Firebase (Google)** — our backend infrastructure provider. Firebase handles account authentication (email/password, verification) and stores your account and profile data (Firestore/Realtime Database) and any images you upload (Firebase Storage). Google acts as our data processor for this purpose. See Section 7 for where this data is processed.
- **Other users**, limited to the public profile and Listing information described in Section 5;
- **Authorities**, where required by law.

We do not use any other third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks at this time. We do not share your data with Instagram or Meta; clicking through to a user's Instagram profile is simply a link, and any data you share there is governed by Instagram's own privacy policy.

## 7. International Data Transfers

GuestMe serves users across the EEA and the UK. Our backend runs on Firebase, provided by Google. Depending on which Firebase server region we use, your data may be processed outside the EEA/UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, Google provides safeguards for such transfers, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, under Google's data processing terms for Firebase/Google Cloud.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data immediately, except where we are required to retain certain data for legal, tax, or security purposes.

## 9. Your Rights

Under GDPR/UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten");
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing;
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a portable format;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent;
- **Lodge a complaint** with your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@guestme.eu. You can also update or delete most profile information (bio, photos, location, real name) directly from your account settings, and delete Listings at any time.

## 10. Cookies

We use only essential cookies/local storage required for core functionality, such as keeping you logged in via Firebase Authentication. We do not use analytics, marketing, or tracking cookies. Because we only use strictly necessary cookies, no cookie consent banner is required under EU/UK rules — but if this changes in the future (e.g., if analytics is added), this section and a consent mechanism will need to be updated.

## 11. Children

GuestMe is intended for professional tattoo artists and studios aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

## 12. Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure, including secure password handling (we never see your raw password), access-controlled databases, and industry-standard encryption in transit. No system is fully secure, so we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

## 13. Third-Party Links

Our Platform links to Instagram profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Instagram or any other third-party site you visit from GuestMe.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes (e.g., by email or in-app notice) before they take effect.

## 15. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support at info@guestme.eu.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
